# socarrat

## Translations
- **DE:** knusprige Reisschicht am Pfannenboden
- **EN:** crispy toasted rice layer

## Grammar
- **Type:** noun (loanword from Valencian)
- **Gender:** masculine (el socarrat)
- **Note:** Mostly used for paella and other rice dishes cooked in a wide pan.

## Example sentences
- El mejor bocado de la paella es el socarrat.
- Si escuchas un crujido suave, se está formando el socarrat.
- Esta vez el socarrat se quemó un poco.

## Mnemonic
"So-carr-at": the rice is so charred at the bottom — but only just. Too far and you get [[pf_2026-03-22_first-burnt-socarrat]].
