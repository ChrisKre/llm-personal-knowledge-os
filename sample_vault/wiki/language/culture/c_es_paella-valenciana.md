# Paella valenciana

## Summary
Paella valenciana is the traditional rice dish of the Valencia region, cooked in a wide, shallow pan (the paellera) over an open flame. The classic version uses rice, chicken, rabbit, green beans, butter beans, olive oil, tomato and [[v_es_azafran]] — no seafood and certainly no chorizo. It is traditionally a Sunday family lunch, often cooked outdoors and eaten straight from the pan. The prized crispy bottom layer is called [[v_es_socarrat]].
